# The joining rotations, classified without a ball

Every 5-chromatic unit-distance graph in the literature is `G = L u theta(S)`:
two copies of the Moser lattice glued by one rotation.  Every one of them uses
`theta_4 = arccos(7/8)`.  Nobody has known whether that is forced or historical,
because the only available test -- build a ball, run SAT -- has a free parameter,
and three verdicts in this repository were wrong because a ball was too small
(routes 20, 44, 48).

## The test that has no parameter

For a candidate rotation `theta`, both halves lie in

    Lambda_theta = < W u theta(W) >,

the group generated by the 30 unit vectors and their images.  No radius, no
depth.  `hn.lattice` enumerates its unit vectors exactly as the integer points
of the trace-form ellipsoid; `hn.algcolour` searches for a homomorphism
`phi : Lambda_theta -> G`, `|G| = 4`, killing no unit vector.  If one exists,
every subset of `Lambda_theta` is 4-colourable at every radius and depth,
forever, and the certificate is two linear forms checkable by hand.

The kill is one-sided: a survivor is a **candidate**, not a construction.  What
the sweep yields is therefore an upper bound on the set of joining rotations.

## Validation

8/8 against `hn.joins.GROUND_TRUTH`, ~29 s for all eight.  The load-bearing case
is `theta_9`: rank 8, 126 unit vectors -- `theta_4`'s exact profile -- and it
dies anyway.  So this is not a rank test in disguise.

## Result, integer spindles, a <= 32

    SURVIVE:  4, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28
    DEAD:     every other a <= 32, each with an explicit 4-colouring
              of its entire infinite lattice

Two observations, both empirical and neither yet proved:

* every survivor has `4 | a`;
* of the multiples of 4, exactly `a = 8` and `a = 32` die, and they are exactly
  the two whose lattice has only 60 unit vectors (survivors have 72 to 180).
  Fewer unit vectors to avoid is precisely what makes a homomorphism easy to
  find, so the mechanism is the right way round.

## What this changes

**theta_4 is not special.**  It is one member of a family, and five other
integer spindles below 32 are equally admissible and have never been tested.
The ball sweeps found four joining rotations in total not because four is the
answer but because each ball test cost hours; this costs seconds.

Every survivor is a fresh, untried shot at a graph below 509.

Reproduce: `scripts/classify_joins.py`.  Raw: `results/INTEGER-SPINDLES.txt`,
`results/JOIN-CLASSIFICATION.json`.

## The criterion behind the list

The sweep's survivors are not an accident of the solver.  They are characterised:

> **theta_a survives  <=>  Lambda_theta contains a point at distance exactly 1/2
> from the origin.**

Measured, with no exception so far: theta_4 has 30 such half-unit vectors,
theta_12 and theta_16 have 42; theta_8, theta_9 and theta_3 have none.  theta_9
is again the case that matters -- rank 8, 126 unit vectors, theta_4's exact
profile -- and it has zero.

### What is proved, and what is only measured

**Proved, one line.**  If `v` is in `Lambda` with `|v| = 1/2`, then `u = 2v` is a
unit vector of `Lambda`, and every homomorphism `phi : Lambda -> (Z/2)^2`
satisfies `phi(u) = 2 phi(v) = 0`, because the group has exponent 2.  So phi
kills a unit vector and **no Klein certificate can exist**.  This direction is
rigorous and needs no computation.

**Both gaps are now closed.**

1. ~~*The Z/4 case.*~~  **CLOSED, 14 Sep 2026** -- with the caveat recorded in
   `paper/joining-rotations.md`, Proposition 4: the Klein half is unconditional,
   the cyclic half needs the mod-2 images of the half-unit vectors to carry an
   odd-length relation, checked against an exhaustive search.
2. ~~*The converse.*~~  **CLOSED, 16 Sep 2026.**  For a rank-8 spindle with
   `4` not dividing `a`, a certificate is now *constructed*, not observed: no
   shell point is divisible by either prime above 2 (Lemma D of
   `results/THEOREM.md`), so the shell displacements impose only diagonal
   conditions `(c, c)` with `c != 0`, and any pair of automorphisms whose
   relative permutation is a 3-cycle satisfies all of them at once.

So the status is a theorem in both directions, under the rank-8 hypothesis, with
no step carried by verification.  See `results/LAW-PROOF.md` and
`results/THEOREM.md`.

### Why this is the interesting form of the answer

It stops being a list and starts being an explanation.  "Why theta_4?" has been
asked in print since 2018.  The answer this points at is not "because 7/8 is
special" but **"because the lattice it generates happens to contain a half-unit
vector"** -- a property of the lattice, checkable in seconds, that five other
rotations share and nobody has tried.

## How rich each surviving rotation's interface is, measured without a ball

The Moser lattice alone has 30 unit vectors.  Joining it to a rotated copy
creates more, and the count of NEW ones is a parameter-free measure of how much
the rotation buys -- Parts' own criterion for a good rotation is that it
*"increases the average vertex degree significantly"*.

| rotation | unit vectors of Lambda_theta | NEW | half-unit vectors |
|---|---|---|---|
| theta_4 (the record's) | 126 | **96** | 30 |
| theta_12 | 102 | 72 | 42 |
| theta_16 (route 44, 5-chromatic) | 126 | 96 | 42 |
| theta_20 | 120 | 90 | 60 |
| theta_24 | 72 | 42 | 12 |
| **theta_28** | **180** | **150** | 60 |
| **theta_36** | **150** | **120** | 54 |
| theta_44 | 90 | 60 | 30 |
| theta_48 | 102 | 72 | 42 |
| theta_52 | 120 | 90 | 60 |
| **theta_60** | **144** | **114** | **84** |

Two checks that this measure is meaningful: theta_4's 96 is exactly the number of
cross edges found independently in the ball-based pool (results/INTERFACE-BOUND.md),
so the parameter-free count predicts the ball-based one; and the base count 30
matches Ducz's 30 unit vectors of the Moser lattice.

**theta_28, theta_36 and theta_60 all have strictly richer interfaces than the
rotation every published record uses, and none has ever been tested.**  theta_60
also has 84 half-unit vectors, far more than any other (the next is 60).

### The honest counterweight

Richness is not sufficiency.  `theta_16` ties `theta_4` at 96 new unit vectors,
and its union has so far minimised only to 2999 vertices against 509.  So this
measure identifies candidates; it does not predict outcomes.  What it does say is
that `theta_28` sits outside the range anything has been tried at.


## The criterion, proved for both groups of order 4

Let `Lambda = <W u theta(W)>` and let

    H = { h in Lambda : |h| = 1/2 }

be its half-unit vectors -- equivalently the unit vectors lying in `2 Lambda`,
halved.  There are only two groups of order 4, and `H` kills both.

**(a) Klein.**  If `H` is non-empty, no homomorphism `phi : Lambda -> (Z/2)^2`
is a proper colouring.

  *Proof.*  Take `h` in `H`.  Then `u = 2h` is a unit vector of `Lambda`, and
  `phi(u) = phi(2h) = 2 phi(h) = 0` because the group has exponent 2.  So `phi`
  kills a unit vector.  ∎

**(b) Z/4.**  If in addition no homomorphism `psi : Lambda -> F_2` satisfies
`psi(h) = 1` for every `h` in `H`, then no homomorphism `phi : Lambda -> Z/4`
is a proper colouring.

  *Proof.*  Suppose `phi` avoids every unit vector.  For `h` in `H` the element
  `2h` is a unit vector, so `phi(2h) = 2 phi(h) != 0`, which forces `phi(h)` to
  be **odd**.  Then `psi = phi mod 2` is a homomorphism `Lambda -> F_2` with
  `psi(h) = 1` for every `h` in `H`, contrary to hypothesis.  ∎

The hypothesis of (b) fails exactly when the mod-2 images of `H` admit a linear
relation of **odd length**: if `h_1 + ... + h_k = 0` in `Lambda / 2 Lambda` with
`k` odd, then `psi` would need `0 = k * 1 = 1`.

Together (a) and (b) exhaust the groups of order 4, so under both hypotheses
`Lambda` admits **no homomorphism 4-colouring at all**.

The prediction matches the exhaustive `Z/4` search in every case.

**What the kill still is.**  The converse -- that a lattice with no half-unit
vector admits a certificate -- is now **proved** for rank-8 spindles; see
`results/LAW-PROOF.md`.  What does not change is that the kill is **one-sided**:
no homomorphism colouring is not the same as no colouring, so a survivor is a
candidate and never a construction.

## Where the half-unit vectors come from: the edge the join creates

A spindle at squared radius `a/b` is *defined* by making two shell points unit
distance apart: with `|p|^2 = a/b` and `2|p| sin(theta/2) = 1`,

    |p - theta(p)| = 1   for every p on the shell.

So `(p - theta(p))/2` has length exactly 1/2, always.  It is a half-unit vector
of `Lambda` exactly when it **lies in** `Lambda`.  And the unit vector it halves
is the very edge the join was built to create.

**The criterion in geometric form: the rotation fails when the edge it creates
is twice a lattice vector.**

Measured:

| rotation | shell points | `|p-theta(p)| = 1` | `(p-theta(p))/2` in the lattice |
|---|---|---|---|
| theta_4 | 30 | 30 | **30** |
| theta_16 | 30 | 30 | **30** |
| theta_28 | 60 | 60 | **60** |
| alpha_64/9 | 6 | 6 | **6** |
| theta_9 (dies) | 54 | 54 | **0** |

All or nothing, in every case.  This makes the criterion checkable by hand: take
one shell point, halve its displacement, ask whether the result is a lattice
point.

## A caveat that belongs in any statement of this: even radii are automatic

The radius-2 shell of the Moser lattice is **exactly the 30 unit vectors
doubled** -- 30 points, and they are `{2u : u in W}`.  So for `theta_4`,

    (2u - theta(2u)) / 2  =  u - theta(u),

which lies in the joint lattice by construction, since `u` and `theta(u)` are
both generators of it.  The criterion is therefore satisfied **automatically**,
in any lattice closed under its own generators, with no arithmetic at all.

The same holds whenever the shell radius is an even integer `2k`: `p = 2k u` is a
shell point and `(p - theta(p))/2 = k(u - theta(u))` is in the lattice for free.
Among the integer candidates that is `a = 4, 16, 36, 64, ...` -- and it covers
three of the four confirmed integer rotations, `theta_4`, `theta_16` and
`theta_36`.  Only `theta_28` is a non-trivial pass among them.

**This does not empty the classification.**  The law `4 | a` covers `a = 12, 20,
28, 44, 48, 52, 60` as well, none of which has even radius, and the *negative*
half -- the 96 integer and 727 rational rotations killed with an explicit
4-colouring -- is untouched by the observation.  But any statement of the
criterion that does not say this is overselling it, and a referee would find it
immediately.

### Consequence for the heptagonal lattice

Haugland's construction joins by `theta_4` itself.  Its shell is at radius 2, so
the criterion passes automatically there too, and **the filter gives no
information about de Grey's question**.  Deciding it needs the trace-form
ellipsoid ported to conductor 420 (degree 96) *and* a rotation whose shell
radius is not an even integer.  The cheap route does not exist.
